### Management context

Nova Precision Components operates a five-day, two-shift production system serving several industrial customers with different priorities and delivery commitments. 
The production system includes:


<div class="three-cols">

<div>• cutting,</div>
<div>• milling,</div>
<div>• heat treatment,</div>
<div>• finishing,</div>
<div>• multiple machines with different capabilities and efficiencies,</div>
<div>• specialised operators,</div>
<div>• planned maintenance,</div>
<div>• sequence-dependent setups,</div>
<div>• material-availability constraints,</div>
<div>• energy-price variation,</div>
<div>• customer penalties for late delivery.</div>

</div>

At the beginning of the challenge, the company already has a weekly production plan generated by a simple legacy heuristic. 
However, the plan may not be fully feasible and may not make effective use of the available resources.

**Your team is asked to redesign the schedule.**

<div style="font-size:0.82em">

  ### Required work by Groups

Management wants to know **What production schedule should Nova execute during the next five working days?**
Your solution should answer four related questions:

  <div>

  <div>• Is the proposed schedule feasible?</div>
  <div>• Does it improve the baseline plan?</div>
  <div>• Which trade-offs does it make between service, cost, utilisation and energy?</div>
  <div>• How robust is it when operating conditions change?</div>

  </div>

Your goal is to develop a better production plan using AI-assisted analysis.
_*You are not expected simply to ask an AI system to “create a schedule”*_.

You are expected to design a complete and defensible approach that combines:
**data preparation → problem formulation → constraint modelling → scheduling logic → validation → comparison → management recommendation**.

</div>

::right::

<div style="height: 10px;"></div>

<div style="font-size:0.82em">

### Tools

You may use:

<div style="font-size:0.7em;margin-left:40pt;">

<div>• Microsoft Copilot Chat,</div>
<div>• Google AI Studio,</div>
<div>• spreadsheets,</div>
<div>• Python or another scripting language,</div>
<div>• AI-generated code,</div>
<div>• heuristics,</div>
<div>• optimisation models,</div>
<div>• or hybrid approaches.</div>

</div>

Notice that:
- A sophisticated optimisation model is not mandatory.
- A valid, reproducible and well-tested heuristic may outperform a poorly validated optimisation approach.
- The data package can intentionally contain data-quality problems. Therefore, cleaning the input data is part of the challenge. Keep record logs of changes made.

</div>

  ### Suggested action plan (Not mandatory)

<div style="margin-left:20pt;">

  <div>• <b>Stage 1: Understand the production system</b> (Before trying to schedule anything, reconstruct the production problem.)</div>
  <div>• <b>Stage 2: Define success before optimising</b> (Before building the scheduling solution, define your success criteria.)</div>
  <div>• <b>Stage 3: Define your scheduling strategy</b> (Your team must design an explicit scheduling approach.)</div>
  <div>• <b>Stage 4: Use AI as an engineering assistant</b> (AI can support different parts of the challenge.)</div>
  <div>• <b>Stage 5: Build the schedule</b> (Your final schedule must follow the supplied submission format.)</div>
  <div>• <b>Stage 6: Validate before comparing</b> (A schedule that looks good but violates constraints is not a good schedule. Your team should therefore build a validation layer.)</div>
  <div>• <b>Stage 7: Compare against the baseline</b> (You must demonstrate whether your plan improves the existing plan.)</div>

</div>

::right::

<div style="height: 20px;"></div>

### Assessment criteria.

<div style="font-size:0.72em">

| **Criterion**                                      | **Weight** |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -----: |
| Problem formulation and managerial relevance       |    15% |
| Definition and justification of success criteria   |    10% |
| Prompting and AI-interaction design                |    15% |
| Data and evidence preparation                      |    15% |
| Analytical, scripting or workflow quality          |    15% |
| Validation, testing and critical assessment        |    15% |
| Feasibility and usefulness for decision-making     |    10% |
| Traceability, responsible AI use and communication |     5% |

</div>

<div style="height: 20px;"></div>

### Additional Notes.

It is expected that your team:
- identify the real scheduling bottlenecks;
- clean the data systematically;
- separate hard and soft constraints correctly;
- develop reproducible scheduling logic;
- use AI intelligently rather than blindly;
- validate independently what AI produced;
- achieve good operational performance;
- understand the trade-offs among competing objectives;
- react effectively to disruption;
- explain the result in terms understandable to management.
